# Levels

Level pre-production — sketches, pacing ideas, and world/country themes **before** they become
formal level scripts. The authoritative level contract is
[`level-script-schema.md`](../level-script-schema.md); do not restate the schema here — link to it.

> Pre-production ideation, not authority. A level graduates from a sketch here to a real script
> authored against the schema. Palette/obstacle rules are LOCKED in
> [`creative-bible.md`](../creative-bible.md).
