# Characters

Characters, mascots, and narrators for the paper-craft world. Concepts only — final visual and
tone rules are governed by [`creative-bible.md`](../creative-bible.md) (LOCKED look/feel/tone).

> Pre-production ideation, not authority. Keep every character inside the bible's tone words
> (_warm, tactile, calm-but-purposeful, handmade, uncluttered_) and anti-words (_harsh, neon,
> busy, glossy-plastic, aggressive_).
